About Techfest

Twenty-nine years of building the future.

Techfest is the annual science and technology festival of IIT Bombay — student-run since 1998 and now the largest of its kind in Asia.

The story

Run by students, staged for everyone.

What began as a small campus gathering has become a temporary city: competition arenas, lecture halls, an exhibition floor, workshops and three nights of spectacle. Every edition is designed and delivered by a student team at IIT Bombay, with the festival's home on the Powai campus at the centre of it all.

The 2026 edition, An Astral Experience, reimagines the campus as a living observatory and the festival as an ecosystem — from the arena floor to the exhibition hall, from the dais to the lawns after dark.
